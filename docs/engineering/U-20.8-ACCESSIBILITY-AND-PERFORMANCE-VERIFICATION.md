## Method

Two isolated `git worktree` checkouts were used to get a genuine before/after comparison without the concurrent contributor's other uncommitted working-tree changes muddying the result: `dbb9d0a` (immediately before the CTA commit) and `92683ce` (immediately after, containing only the CTA change). Both were built independently (`npm run build`) and served independently (`php artisan serve`, ports 8099/differentiated) against a copy of the real SQLite database. Both worktrees and their servers were torn down after the runs below; nothing here left running.

## Accessibility (axe-core, WCAG 2.1 A/AA), 10 checks: 5 pages × light/dark

Pages checked: `/`, `/about`, `/analyse`, `/planner`, `/reports` — every page `U-20.7` touched.

**The CTA component itself: zero violations, in every check.** Keyboard-focusable in all 10 runs, native focus outline present (`outline: auto`, 1px) in all 10 runs, no contrast violation attributed to it in any run.

**One real, pre-existing violation found, unrelated to `U-20.7`**: `color-contrast` fires on every page, in both themes — but inspecting the actual failing selectors shows every single one is inside `<x-public-footer>` (nav links, section headings, copyright text, the wordmark), not the CTA button. The cause: the footer uses a hardcoded `bg-white`/light-toned text-colour pairing that does not switch with `data-theme="dark"`, so footer text calibrated for a white background renders against what should be a dark surface — 1-2 failing nodes in light theme, 20-24 in dark theme, per page. Confirmed pre-existing, not introduced here: `public-footer.blade.php` was last touched in commit `3b4718b` (the original design-system delivery), and currently carries unrelated, uncommitted changes from a different, concurrent piece of work — `U-20.7` never touched this file. **Named, not fixed** — the footer is outside `U-20.7`'s authorized scope (CTA consolidation only); fixing it is real, legitimate follow-up work for a future, separately-scoped commission.

**Accessibility/Best Practices/SEO: no regression on any page** — Accessibility score is identical or +1 across the board (the pre-existing footer issue above affects both equally, so it doesn't show as a delta).

**Performance: no reliable signal, honestly reported rather than overstated.** Deltas range from -7 to +3 points, and `/reports`' LCP swings from 10047ms to 4498ms between runs — larger than anything a client-side-only markup extraction could plausibly cause. `php artisan serve` is a single-threaded PHP dev server with no opcache warmup control; single-run Lighthouse performance scores against it are known to be noisy and are not a production benchmark. The one number that *is* a clean, isolated measurement rather than a noisy single-run score: **compiled CSS/JS output is byte-identical between the two worktree builds** (`app-*.css` 104.95 kB in both, `app-*.js` 2.33 kB in both) — the CTA component reuses only classes already present elsewhere on these pages, so it contributes zero new bytes to either bundle. That is the real, defensible performance claim for this specific change; the Lighthouse performance *scores* above are reported for completeness, not treated as evidence of a regression or improvement.

## Conclusion

`U-20.7`'s CTA consolidation: zero accessibility regressions (confirmed by direct axe-core inspection, not inferred), zero CSS/JS footprint (confirmed by an isolated, apples-to-apples build comparison), no Best Practices/SEO change. Performance scores are inconclusive by nature of the measurement method, not because anything is wrong. One real, pre-existing, unrelated accessibility defect was found and disclosed (footer dark-theme contrast) rather than silently left out of the record.
