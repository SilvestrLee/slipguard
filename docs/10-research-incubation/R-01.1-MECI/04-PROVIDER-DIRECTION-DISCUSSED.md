# Provider Direction Discussed — MECI

Following the research in `02-PARSER-OFFICE-PHASE-0.md` and `03-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW.md`, the direction discussed was:

- **Primary football-context provider**: SportMonks — strongest architectural fit, comprehensive coverage across the needed families in its base tier, compatible commercial licensing model. Conditional on the written retention clarification named in `03-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW.md` §6 actually being sent and answered before any production reliance.
- **Weather provider**: Visual Crossing — compatible licensing, derived-result redistribution explicitly supported, lower compliance risk than the alternative. Conditional on the written tier-storage clarification in the same section.
- **Secondary provider**: football-data.org, approved-in-discussion only for standings/league-table/supplementary context. Any expanded use would need the same full-ToS review SportMonks and Visual Crossing already received.
- **Explicitly not directed toward**: OpenWeatherMap's self-service tier, due to the ODbL share-alike finding in `03-COMPLIANCE-REVIEW.md`. Its Enterprise tier was left open as a possible future alternative, untested here.
