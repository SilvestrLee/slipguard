# Feature Gating

## Public
Educational pages, product explanation, and registration.

## Registered User
Manual analysis, saved reports, history, journal, profile, and — behind `MARKET_WIDE_PLANNER_ENABLED` pending Product Office's own public-launch authorization — Build an Accumulator (Capability B) and its conversational natural-language entry layer.

## Premium Candidates — Post-MVP
Higher usage limits, longer history, real OCR (image-to-text extraction), and advanced explanations, behaviour insights.
