# Feature Gating

## Public
Educational pages, product explanation, registration, and Contact.

## Registered User
Manual analysis, saved reports, history, journal, profile, Settings, Help & Methodology, and — behind `MARKET_WIDE_PLANNER_ENABLED`, launch-enabled per `PO-MVP-004` — Build an Accumulator (Capability B) and its conversational natural-language entry layer.

## Premium Candidates — Post-MVP
Higher usage limits, longer history, real OCR (image-to-text extraction), and advanced explanations, behaviour insights.
