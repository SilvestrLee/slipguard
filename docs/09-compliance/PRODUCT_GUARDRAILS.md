# Product Guardrails

## Prohibited Claims
Guaranteed wins, safe bets, certain outcomes, guaranteed profit, or elimination of betting risk.

## Responsible Design
Avoid urgency designed to increase betting frequency, loss-chasing prompts, manipulative streaks, casino-style pressure, or language that treats a risk score as permission to bet.

## Transparency
Every report should state that SlipGuard evaluates structural risk, does not predict outcomes, sport remains uncertain, and the user owns the final decision.

## Operator & Custody Boundaries (added `ADR-012`, 2026-07-27)
SlipGuard never holds customer funds — no deposits, wallets, withdrawals, escrow, or bet settlement; every financial transaction stays exclusively between customer and licensed operator. SlipGuard never places, increases, or executes a wager autonomously — every wager requires a conscious customer action. No affiliate agreement, bookmaker partnership, or commercial incentive may bias a deterministic calculation, risk classification, or recommendation. Any proposal introducing fund custody, autonomous execution, or bookmaker-like behaviour is escalated to Product Office before development begins. Full record: `docs/adr/ADR-012-PRODUCT-BOUNDARIES-OPERATOR-INDEPENDENCE-AND-REGULATORY-POSITIONING.md`.

Final legal and regulatory wording requires review before public launch.
