# Product Guardrails

## Prohibited Claims
Guaranteed wins, safe bets, certain outcomes, guaranteed profit, or elimination of betting risk.

## Responsible Design
Avoid urgency designed to increase betting frequency, loss-chasing prompts, manipulative streaks, casino-style pressure, or language that treats a risk score as permission to bet.

## Transparency
Every report should state that SlipGuard evaluates structural risk, does not predict outcomes, sport remains uncertain, and the user owns the final decision.

Final legal and regulatory wording requires review before public launch.
