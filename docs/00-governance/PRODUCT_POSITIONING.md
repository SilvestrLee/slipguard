# Product Positioning

## Category Statement
SlipGuard is a betting risk intelligence platform for bettors who want to understand structural weaknesses in a betting slip before placing it.

## Core Promise
SlipGuard explains what is risky, why it matters, which leg contributes the most risk, and what the user should review next.

## Differentiation
Prediction products ask: “Who will win?”

SlipGuard asks: “Should this selection be on your ticket, and what unnecessary risk does it introduce?”

## Prohibited Positioning
Do not describe SlipGuard as a prediction website, tips platform, AI picks generator, guaranteed betting system, or safe-bet generator.
