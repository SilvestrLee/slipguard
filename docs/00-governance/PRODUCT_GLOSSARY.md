# Product Glossary

## Analysis
A versioned evaluation of a submitted betting slip.

## Betting Slip
A collection of one or more betting selections.

## Leg
One selection within a betting slip.

## Risk Score
A deterministic representation of structural decision risk under the active engine version.

## Risk Band
A plain-language category derived from the risk score.

## Weakest Leg
The leg contributing the greatest explainable structural risk. It is not a prediction that the leg will lose.

## Accumulator Tax
The compounding structural risk created by combining multiple selections.

## Confidence
Normally refers to data quality or analysis completeness, not confidence that an outcome will occur.

## Journal
A simple record of what the user decided and what they later learned.
