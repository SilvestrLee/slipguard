# SlipGuard Project Charter

## Purpose
SlipGuard helps bettors identify unnecessary structural risk before placing a bet.

## Category
Betting Risk Intelligence.

## Product Boundary
SlipGuard evaluates decision risk. It does not predict sporting outcomes.

## Founder Directive
Build the original useful product without feature bloat, false certainty, or unnecessary engineering complexity.

## Operating Principles
- Trust before engagement.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Explainability before sophistication.
- Delivery before excessive documentation.
- One stable monolith before distributed systems.
- User decision quality before bet volume.

## Governance
Major product or architecture changes require explicit founder approval and a durable decision record when they affect future implementation.
