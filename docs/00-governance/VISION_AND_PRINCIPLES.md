# SlipGuard Vision & Principles

## Vision
To become the world’s most trusted betting risk intelligence platform.

## Mission
To help bettors identify unnecessary risk before placing a bet through transparent, explainable, and responsible analysis.

## SlipGuard Is
- A betting risk intelligence platform.
- A slip analysis tool.
- A decision-support system.
- A learning and discipline aid.

## SlipGuard Is Not
- A bookmaker.
- A tipster.
- A prediction engine.
- A guaranteed winning system.
- An odds comparison service.
- A gambling recommendation platform.

## Principles
1. Trust above all.
2. Explain every important result.
3. Evidence over opinion.
4. Never manufacture certainty.
5. Responsible product design.
6. Simplicity wins.
7. AI assists; deterministic rules decide.
8. Build only what solves a real problem.
9. Deliver progressively.
10. Maintainability is a product feature.
