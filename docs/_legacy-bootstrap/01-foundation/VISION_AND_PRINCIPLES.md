# SlipGuard Vision & Principles

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved foundation  
**Owner:** Product Office

## Vision

To become the world’s most trusted betting risk intelligence platform.

## Mission

To help bettors identify unnecessary risk before placing a bet through transparent, explainable, and responsible analysis.

## The Problem

Bettors have access to odds, tips, statistics, communities, and predictions, yet often cannot answer:

> Is this betting slip structurally sensible, and where is its avoidable risk?

Many losses involve preventable decision errors:

- Chasing combined odds.
- Adding unnecessary legs.
- Underestimating accumulator compounding.
- Misreading market risk.
- Ignoring weak selections.
- Confusing confidence with probability.
- Repeating poor betting habits.

SlipGuard addresses decision quality, not match prediction.

## Product Identity

SlipGuard is:

- A betting risk intelligence platform.
- A slip analysis tool.
- A decision-support system.
- A learning and discipline aid.

SlipGuard is not:

- A bookmaker.
- A tipster.
- A prediction engine.
- A guaranteed winning system.
- An odds comparison site.
- A gambling recommendation platform.

## Principles

### VP-001 — Trust Above All

Never trade long-term trust for clicks, conversion, or exaggerated claims.

### VP-002 — Explain Every Important Result

A user should understand what was found, why it matters, and what to review.

### VP-003 — Evidence Over Opinion

Business-critical conclusions come from deterministic rules and auditable data.

### VP-004 — No False Certainty

Sport is uncertain. SlipGuard must never present probability or risk as a guarantee.

### VP-005 — Responsible Product Design

Do not use urgency, casino aesthetics, manipulative streaks, or features designed to increase betting frequency.

### VP-006 — Simplicity Wins

Complex analysis should produce clear language, restrained interfaces, and obvious next actions.

### VP-007 — AI Assists; Rules Decide

AI may explain verified results. It must not generate the risk score, weakest leg, confidence, or mathematical verdict.

### VP-008 — Build Only What Solves a Real Problem

Every feature must have a clear user problem, measurable value, and place in the roadmap.

### VP-009 — Progressive Delivery

Launch a focused useful product, then expand using user evidence.

### VP-010 — Maintainability Is a Product Feature

Prefer Laravel conventions, explicit domain services, tests, and minimal dependencies.

## Decision Filter

A feature should proceed only when it:

1. Improves betting decision quality.
2. Increases clarity or trust.
3. Can be explained.
4. Supports responsible use.
5. Fits the current release objective.
6. Can be maintained without disproportionate complexity.
